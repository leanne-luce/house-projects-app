`PlanCard` is the catalogue unit on clubluce.com. Lay them out in a 3-up grid with `gap: 3rem 2.25rem`.

```jsx
<PlanCard tag="Woodwork" title="Low Long Shelf" description="A low, long shelf that lines a wall." image="assets/photos/basic-finished-image.png" />
```

The card itself is unstyled — no box. The image does the work.
